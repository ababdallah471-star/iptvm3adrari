Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) is a digital television service delivered over the internet. Instead of traditional cable or satellite, you stream live TV channels and on-demand content directly through your internet connection.

Our service works on Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony), streaming devices (Firestick, Android TV, Apple TV), smartphones and tablets (iOS & Android), computers (Windows, Mac), and set-top boxes.

We recommend at least 10 Mbps for HD streaming and 25 Mbps for 4K content. The service automatically adjusts quality based on your available bandwidth to ensure smooth playback.

Simply choose a plan, complete the payment, and we'll send you your login credentials via email within minutes. Follow the setup guide to install the app on your device and start streaming.
